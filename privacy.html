Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

What we collect

Your name and email address when you buy, your licence key, and the technical information needed to verify your licence. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see your card details.

Your music, presets, recordings and settings stay on your device. Rigsta has no ads and no tracking.

How we use it

Only to deliver your licence, verify it, and give you support. We do not sell or share your data, except with the service providers needed to run Rigsta or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at rigstalivearranger@gmail.com. Deleting your data ends your licence.
