Explore and hear your sets.
Merge, convert, and play them.

Rigsta opens your Korg Pa4X and Pa5X sets so you can explore and hear every sound and sample inside them, merge and convert between sets, organise your SongBook, and turn your PC, tablet or phone into a live controller with harmony and tuning.

Merge

Drag styles, keyboard sets, pads and sounds from one set into another. The sounds, samples, STS and pads an item needs travel with it, and a sound already there is not copied twice.

How to merge →

Convert Pa4X → Pa5X

Convert the set you are working on, or convert a Pa4X set in place and drag the parts you want into your Pa5X set. The Pa4X folder you open is never changed.

How to convert →

Explore & hear

Every sound drawn as its shape on a piano. Every sample, zone and drum key plays at a tap, coloured by where it lives in the set.

Explore a set →

SongBook

One list of your songs, searchable by name, artist, genre and style. Add a song from a style, set its key, meter and tempo, and build set lists for the night.

Open the SongBook →

Live Control

Every style a tap away, with STS, elements and transport on one deck you can arrange to your liking. The lights follow the keyboard.

Play live →

Harmony

A scale-true second voice from your melody. Use the built-in harmonies, or build and save your own in any key. The melody is never delayed; only the harmony passes through the app.

Add harmony →

Tuning

Oriental and Balkan scales and quarter tones, sent to the keyboard in one tap. Save your own scales, each on its own key. Clear returns to equal temperament.

Tune it →

PC, tablet or phone

A layout made for each screen. A rail of pages on a PC and tablet; the stage pages as tabs at your thumbs on a phone. Long-press for the copy menu, swipe through the styles.

What each screen shows, and every gesture →

Connect in three steps

  1. USB cable from the keyboard's USB Device port to your PC, tablet or phone.
  2. On the keyboard: build the Rigsta MIDI preset (one preset for every page — see the manual), Clock Send on.
  3. In Rigsta: Settings → KEYBOARD → choose To keyboard and From keyboard. The dot turns green.
Full MIDI setup guide →

Download Rigsta

Rigsta installs free and opens in Free Audition mode: audition Pa5X styles on your keyboard. Buy a licence and enter your key in the app to unlock merging, conversion, sound editing and every page.

Windows installer Android app (APK)
  • Windows: run Rigsta-Setup.exe. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, choose More info → Run anyway.
  • Android: open Rigsta.apk on your phone or tablet. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to Install unknown apps (Settings → Apps → that app → Install unknown apps), then tap Install. A newer version installs over the old one and keeps your licence.
  • Buying: inside Rigsta, under Purchase a License. Rigsta first checks that your PC, phone or tablet meets the requirements, so you never pay for a device it cannot run on. Your licence key is shown right after you pay and sent to you by email.

All versions and SHA-256 checksums

System requirements

  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with TPM 2.0 enabled
  • Android: Android 14 or newer, 64-bit phone or tablet; some devices lack the security hardware Rigsta needs
  • A Korg Pa4X or Pa5X keyboard (USB) and an Internet connection

Install Rigsta and try it in Free Audition mode before you buy.
